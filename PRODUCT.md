# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no build step. Hosted on Cloudflare Workers static assets (served from `public/`, deploys on every commit to main).

## Users
Two equal audiences: (1) parents of school students (AP, IB, IGCSE computer science) deciding whether to book a first call, and (2) university students and career-changers (Security+, CCNA, Python, Java, cloud and DevSecOps). Visitors often already know the owner from Preply, or arrive from search, YouTube or Instagram, and want to judge quickly whether he is credible and easy to book.

## Product Purpose
A personal site for Syed Shahmikh Ali ("Shahmikh"), a one-to-one tutor and mentor, whose job is to lead visitors to book a free 15-minute strategy call (Calendly). Success: a visitor understands who he teaches, sees real proof, and books or sends a message.

## Positioning
One person, live one-to-one teaching across school computer science (AP CSP, AP CSA, IB CS, IGCSE CS), certification and tech subjects (Security+, CCNA, Python, Java), plus honest study-strategy and career guidance, taught by a mechatronics engineer who moved into cloud and security.

## Operating Context
Sessions are live 1:1 video lessons for students in the US, UK and Europe. The owner is based in Pakistan. Booking is by Calendly embed on the contact page; the contact form posts to Formspree. A hello@shahmikh.com address is being set up.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Honest tone: no grade or job guarantees, no outcome promises.
- Pricing ranges on the current site are placeholders and unconfirmed. Do not show prices as fact.
- Unverified, do not state as fact: "AWS Cloud Engineer", "97 reviews", "4,500+ hours taught", "9 tracks".
- Social links (YouTube, Instagram) stay hidden until real URLs exist.

## Brand Commitments
- Name and mark: SHAHMIKH wordmark with the "S" mark; existing brand line "Study smarter. Choose wisely. Build what's next."
- Confirmed credentials: Preply Super Tutor; in the top 15% of computer science tutors on Preply. Mention in plain text only. Do not use Preply's logo, badge artwork, colours, fonts or layout, and do not look like Preply.
- Owner direction (volunteered): the site should feel warm and personal, with his face and voice leading.
- The owner dislikes the site looking "made by Claude" (generic AI look).

## Evidence on Hand
- Real written reviews from Preply students and parents (first names only): 16 on the testimonials page.
- One headshot (`public/headshot.jpg`).
- Absent, do not fabricate: other photos, intro video, case studies, grade or result statistics, screenshots of the Preply profile, social channel URLs.

## Product Principles
1. Proof before pitch: real reviews and the Preply credential sit next to every booking action.
2. One person, one voice: first person, plain, specific; no corporate language.
3. Two audiences, one clear first step: parents and learners each find their path in seconds, both ending at the same call.
4. Never claim more than can be shown.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable on phones (most parents will book from a phone), good contrast, keyboard friendly.
