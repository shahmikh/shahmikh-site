Privacy Policy

What I collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 30 October 2026. This policy explains what personal information shahmikh.com collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers this website only. Lessons booked on Preply are governed by Preply's own terms and privacy policy.

Who is responsible

The site is run by Syed Shahmikh Ali, an independent tutor based in Pakistan ("I", "me"). To ask anything about your data, use the contact form.

What I collect, and why

  • Contact form (Contact page). Your name, email, the topic you pick and your message. I use it only to reply to you. It is handled by Formspree, a form service.
  • Free guide sign-up. Your email address and whether you are a student, parent or professional. I use it to send you the guide and, if you agree by signing up, occasional study tips and resources. The list is held by Brevo, an email service. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Kids AI Program waitlist. The parent or guardian's name and email, the child's age group, preferred format, time zone or country, and any message. It is for a parent or guardian to fill in. It is used only to contact you about the program, and it is handled by Formspree. Please do not enter a child's name or other details about the child beyond what the form asks.
  • Booking a call. Calls are booked through Calendly, which collects the details you enter there (name, email, time) under its own privacy policy.
  • Visit statistics. I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, pages and countries. It is designed to be privacy-first and does not use cookies or track you across sites.

Children

This site is meant for students' parents, guardians and adult learners. I do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you think a child has sent me information, contact me and I will delete it.

Who else sees your data

Only the service providers named above (Formspree, Brevo, Calendly, Cloudflare) and, for downloads bought on Gumroad, Gumroad, which handles payment itself. I do not sell your personal information or rent it to anyone. These providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States.

Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. The embedded Calendly booking widget on the Contact page may set cookies of its own when it loads.

How long I keep it

Messages and waitlist entries are kept as long as needed to answer you and run the program, then deleted. Email list members stay until they unsubscribe or ask me to delete them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask me to show you, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you, and you can unsubscribe from emails at any time. If you are in the UK or the EU you also have the right to object to or restrict how your data is used and to complain to your local data protection authority. Use the contact form to make a request.

Changes

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top.
